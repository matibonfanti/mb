PORT·FI: fixed-income scenarios and return attribution

September 2026 · interactive tool

home projects reading list

Views on bonds are stated in yields, but outcomes happen in returns, and the translation depends on carry, roll-down, what the forward curve already prices, and when the move happens. PORT·FI makes that explicit: pick a position, drag your expected US Treasury curve (or set a Fed path or an inflation view), and see the return over time split into carry, roll-down, curve change and your view versus the forwards. It also works backwards: what the market has to do to break even, beat cash or hit a target return.

It runs on daily Treasury and TIPS data and prices everything by full cash-flow repricing, with a Python engine that runs in the browser.
